Defending the thesis

A seven-lesson walk through how this framework is built — and how each design choice protects the claim that the LLM agentic method beats heuristic baselines on recovery after disruption.

The claim you're defending. An LLM-driven, decentralized decision agent beats four heuristic baselines on recovery after disruption — while every method sees the same per-UAV observation. Each lesson below hands you one piece of that argument, grounded in the code.
