Lesson 1 — agentic_uav

The whole-system map

One picture of how a tick flows through the framework — and why its shape is the thesis defense.

Mission link. You're learning this to defend the thesis — that the LLM agentic method beats heuristic baselines on recovery after disruption. This lesson gives you the map a reviewer's questions will move across. Every later lesson zooms into one box on it.

The one idea

The framework is a shared experiment harness wrapped around one swappable decision layer. Everything — world, events, communication, sensing, metrics — is held constant across all five methods. Only the box labelled "decide" changes. That single design choice is what lets the paper say the methods differ only in reasoning. Hold this sentence in your head and the rest of the codebase falls into place.

What one tick does

The heartbeat is Simulation.step() simulation.py. Every tick runs the same seven beats, in order:

  1. Apply events. EventInjector fires scheduled disruptions (dropout, block_sector, urgent_sector). Each fired event also calls method.handle_event().
    simulation.py · communication.py
  2. Deliver messages. NetworkModel moves peer messages — range-limited, delayed, TTL-bounded. This is the only way one UAV learns what another knows.
    communication.py
  3. Build observations. ObservationBuilder.build() assembles one observation per UAV from its local view + belief + inbox.
    planning.py
  4. Decide. The selected SwarmMethod.decide_tick() turns observations into a list of Actions. baselines agentic = LLM
    policy.py (+ llm_agent.py for agentic)
  5. Resolve actions. Validate targets, set roles, move one Manhattan step, enqueue outgoing messages.
    simulation.py
  6. Sense. Non-blocked sectors near each UAV get marked covered. This is what writes new knowledge into a UAV's belief next tick.
    simulation.py
  7. Log. MetricsLogger records coverage, active UAVs, messages, and the replan / plan-change traces used for the recovery analysis.
    simulation.py

Notice beats 1–3 and 5–7 are identical for every method. The whole experiment turns on beat 4.

The two invariants you'll defend

The architecture is built to protect two properties. Almost any reviewer attack on the thesis is, underneath, an attack on one of these:

Observation symmetry. All five methods receive the identical per-UAV observation. Grid layout is global; coverage & urgent are discovered locally. So they can only differ in reasoning, never in what they can see.
Decentralization. Every method decides per-UAV from local observation, a per-UAV BeliefMap, peer messages, and onboard state. No global oracle, no central planner — the LLM method included.

The killer question is "isn't the agentic win just a global oracle?" The answer is structural: the old global urgent_cells list was removed, belief was moved onto MethodState (not the world), and the observation is built once and handed to whichever method runs. A UAV out of range with no peer message simply will not know about a far urgent sector — by design. See ADR-0001.
